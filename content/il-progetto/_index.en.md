StamoTenti was born from a simple idea: talking seriously about meditation doesn’t mean choosing between rigor and clarity. Here, we try to bring together science, philosophy, and a critical perspective, without sacrificing any of the three to make the others easier to read quickly.

## How an Article Is Created

We strive to be honest about what is now well-established, what is a plausible interpretation, and what is still just a hypothesis—rather than presenting everything with the same level of certainty. When we cite a source, the link takes you directly to that source so you can verify it for yourself.

## Who’s Behind It

There’s just one person behind StamoTenti. I’m an engineer by training, but my day job is teaching at a high school. I meditate every day, and that’s where the need for a place like this came from.

I’m going back to school to become a psychologist and am pursuing a master’s degree in Contemplative Studies—a way to build a more solid foundation for something I’ve been practicing for years. I’m also certified as a yoga and tai chi instructor, though I don’t currently teach either. I’m studying voice-over work and have done a bit of theater, and over time I hope that will come through in the project’s audio guides as well.

StamoTenti grows in my free time, piece by piece, without rushing.
