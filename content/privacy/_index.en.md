This privacy policy explains what data we collect when you visit StamoTenti, why we collect it, and what rights you have. It is written as simply as possible: if anything is unclear, please write to us (see “Contact” below).

## Data Controller

The data controller is {{% sitedata "titolare.nome" %}}. For any privacy-related inquiries, please write to {{% sitedata "titolare.email" %}}.

## What Kind of Site Is StamoTenti

StamoTenti is a static site: it does not require accounts, logins, or personal areas for readers, and it does not have a public commenting system. This alone limits much of the data that a typical website collects.

## Data Collected When You Browse

StamoTenti is hosted on {{% sitedata "hosting.provider" %}}. Like any website, the server automatically receives certain technical information necessary to display the pages to you (for example, your IP address, browser type, and the page requested); these technical logs are managed directly by {{% sitedata "hosting.provider" %}} in accordance with their privacy policy, not by us.

## Visitor Statistics (Analytics)

We use {{% sitedata "analytics.strumento" %}}, a statistics tool designed not to identify individual users: it does not install cookies, does not save your IP address, and does not cross-reference data with other sites you visit. We use it solely to understand, in aggregate form, which pages are being viewed.

## Donations

If you choose to support the project with a donation, the button will take you to the Ko-fi page, an external service that handles the payment. StamoTenti neither receives nor stores your card or payment method information: this is managed directly by Ko-fi, in accordance with their privacy policy, which we encourage you to read before proceeding.

## Cookies

StamoTenti does not use profiling, marketing, or analytics cookies: neither {{% sitedata "hosting.provider" %}} nor {{% sitedata "analytics.strumento" %}}, as we use them, require a cookie to function.

## Who We Share Data With

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes. The only parties who may have access to technical data are the technical providers who enable the website to function ({{% sitedata "hosting.provider" %}} for hosting, {{% sitedata "analytics.strumento" %}} for statistics, Ko-fi for donations), each in accordance with their respective roles and privacy policies.

## How Long We Retain Data

We retain only the data necessary for the purposes described above, for as long as strictly necessary. {{% sitedata "analytics.strumento" %}} does not retain IP addresses or individual identifiers for more than a few hours. The retention periods for {{% sitedata "hosting.provider" %}}’s technical logs depend on their privacy policy, not on our decision.

## Your rights

Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), you have the right to request access to your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, and to object to its processing where applicable. You can exercise these rights by writing to {{% sitedata "titolare.email" %}}.

## Changes to This Policy

If the tools we use or the purposes of processing change, we will update this page and indicate the date of the last update.
