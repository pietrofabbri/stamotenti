StamoTenti brings together in-depth articles on meditation, mindfulness, and
attention, written to be understood by anyone who is curious—not just
those who have already studied the subject. It’s not a blog that’s updated every
day: it’s a project that grows steadily, one article at a time, without
ever compromising on rigor.

## Three Perspectives

The same topic, viewed from three different angles.

**Science & Neuroscience.** How the mind and body respond to the practice:
attention, physiology, and deep states such as flow.

**Philosophy & History.** How traditions have conceptualized the experience:
language, silence, and the transition from tradition to modernity.

**Criticism, Society & Education.** How meditation fits into contemporary culture:
schools, work, digital media.

## Before You Begin

You don’t need to be an expert in meditation, nor do you need a scientific or
philosophical background: each article starts from scratch and guides readers through
even the most technical aspects, one step at a time.

You won’t find motivational advice or promises of quick transformations—
just what research, history, and practice truly have to say,
limitations included.

It’s free and growing at a steady pace: there’s nothing to sell you on this page.
If you want to get started right away, the [library](/biblioteca/) collects the articles
published so far; if you want to know more about who’s behind it, you’ll find everything in
[the project](/il-progetto/).
