title: "No fireworks"
description: "Why StamoTenti doesn't use infinite scrolling, notifications, pop-ups, or countdowns—it's a choice, not an oversight."
---

You won’t find infinite scrolling, push notifications, pop-ups, or countdowns here. This isn’t an oversight—it’s a choice—and we want to explain why.

StamoTenti is all about meditation, mindfulness, and attention. It seemed only right to us that the site should reflect these values in its design, rather than just writing about them. Many websites are designed to keep you there as long as possible: endless scrolling, notifications, content designed for impulse rather than reading. That’s not how it works here.

## What You Won’t Find

Endless scrolling, content that reloads on its own to keep you glued to the screen, pop-ups that interrupt your reading—none of that here. No push notifications, no countdowns, no badges reminding you how much you’ve missed. There’s no carousel of “articles you might be interested in” designed solely to keep you here, and nothing plays automatically—neither audio nor video.

## What you’ll find instead

Typography designed to be read, not just looked at: consistent font sizes, lines of readable length, and contrast optimized even for those with visual impairments. A minimalist color palette, free of decorative elements that serve no purpose. Navigation stripped down to the essentials—just what you need to find your way around, not a menu designed to make you click more.

Even the visitor statistics we use are designed not to track you: they show which pages are being read, not who you are or what you do elsewhere. Details can be found on the [privacy page](/en/privacy/).

## Why We Can Afford to Do This

Many websites rely on advertising, and the more time you spend scrolling, the more ads you see. StamoTenti has no ads and does not sell your data. It’s supported by donations from readers (if you’d like, you can [support it](https://ko-fi.com/stamotenti)) and, in the future, through courses and live events. We don’t need to keep you glued to the screen: it’s enough for you to find what you’re looking for and then return to your practice, away from here.
